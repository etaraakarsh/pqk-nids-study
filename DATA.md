# Data

The raw datasets are **not** included in this repository. They are publicly available from their original providers, and their licences do not allow us to redistribute them. The derived files (deduplicated splits, preprocessors) are regenerated by `phase0_data_and_baselines.ipynb`.

## Datasets

| Dataset | What it is | Source |
|---|---|---|
| NF-UNSW-NB15-v2 | NetFlow version of UNSW-NB15, 1,986,745 flows | Sarhan et al. (2021, 2022). NetFlow datasets of the University of Queensland, https://staff.itee.uq.edu.au/marius/NIDS_datasets/ . The study used the cleaned Parquet release (`NF-UNSW-NB15-V2.parquet`) |
| UNSW-NB15 | Official training and testing files | Moustafa and Slay (2015, 2016). https://research.unsw.edu.au/projects/unsw-nb15-dataset |
| Fashion-MNIST | Official image files; the study uses the pair T-shirt/top vs Shirt | Xiao et al. (2017). https://github.com/zalandoresearch/fashion-mnist |

Use the official Fashion-MNIST image files. Widely shared CSV copies do not reproduce the official training and test split.

The audit notebook also accepts the folder names exactly as they come out of the providers' zip files. Unzip the Fashion-MNIST `.gz` files, because the hashes below are for the unzipped files.

## What the audit does with the data

Identifiers that cannot generalise (addresses, ports, DNS query IDs, record IDs, TCP sequence numbers) are removed, identical feature vectors are merged, and the splits are drawn so that no feature vector appears in two splits. About 95% of NF-UNSW-NB15-v2 records repeat another record exactly. For UNSW-NB15, a clean test set is built from the official test rows that share no vector with the training file. The details are in Section 4.2 and Appendix A of the paper, and the resulting split hashes are recorded in the Stage 1 freeze.
